### Who is Kaan Kross?

**Kaan Kross** is an independent developer and the founder of the **4R022** brand. He is of half-Turkish, half-Russian origin. His work focuses on **software engineering, system design, data analysis, performance optimization, and digital security**, with an emphasis on technical structures that can interact with one another.

He deliberately does not make his real identity, age, location, or private-life details public. He wants to be evaluated through his public work and the information he chooses to publish.

His working approach rests on analytical thinking, technical discipline, continuity, and long-term planning. For him, making a system work is not enough: how it is designed, how its components interact, what limits it operates within, and how it can be improved over time matter equally.

### What is 4R022?

**4R022** is an independent technology brand founded by Kaan Kross. Under it he develops software projects, system tools, and technical work for a variety of purposes.

- **Public projects** are the part of the brand that meets users and the software community. A significant portion is offered **free of charge** and aims to solve practical, everyday problems.
- **Beyond public projects**, 4R022 is also a working framework that includes development processes, personal work tools, internal technical components, and future project goals. Which parts are published and which stay private is decided deliberately.
- Public projects are the visible part of the brand; they are not obliged to represent it in its entirety.

### Engineering philosophy

- Project value is judged by functionality, stability, performance, maintainability, and how well it lends itself to further development.
- Values technical documentation, version history, bug assessment, testing, and performance improvement.
- Favors modularity and flexibility: optional components instead of mandatory features for every user.
- Measures technical success by how efficiently a system uses its resources, not only by how comprehensive it is.
- In analysis, distinguishes observation from inference and verified information from assumption.

### Principles and boundaries

- **Privacy:** Personal information that has not been published is not public, and not publishing it does not grant permission to investigate or publish it.
- **Criticism is welcome:** Reviewing, evaluating, and criticizing the published projects and technical decisions is natural and valued.
- **Authorization:** Kaan Kross is the founder and sole authorized administrator of 4R022's private systems. Access to a public repository does not grant authority over other accounts, servers, files, or unpublished projects.
- **Conduct:** Mutual respect is expected. Harassment, threats, deliberate harm, unauthorized access, and violations of personal privacy are not accepted.
- **Slogan:** *Independent Vision. Controlled Access. Uncompromising Principles.*
