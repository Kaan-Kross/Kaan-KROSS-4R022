## 01 · ABOUT THE FOUNDER

I am **Kaan Kross**. I am of half-Turkish, half-Russian origin, and I am the founder of the **4R022** brand.

I choose not to make my personal identity, age, location, and certain details of my private life public. This choice reflects **the importance I place on personal privacy** and the boundaries I maintain between my professional work and my private life.

I prefer to be known through my public identity, my work, and the information I choose to publish. Not sharing details about my private life does not prevent my work from being evaluated. Likewise, a developer presenting their projects to the public does not need to disclose their personal life in full.

I do not define myself merely as someone who builds applications. My interests focus on **software engineering, system design, data analysis, performance optimization, digital security**, and technical structures capable of interacting with one another.

For me, simply making a system work is not enough. How the system is designed, how its components interact, within which limits it operates, and how it can be improved over time are equally important.

My working approach is built on **analytical thinking, technical discipline, continuity, and long-term planning**.

---

## 02 · WHAT IS 4R022?

**4R022** is the independent technology brand I founded.

Under this brand, I develop software projects, system tools, and technical work for a variety of purposes. These projects are not limited to meeting a single need; they also reflect my approach to software architecture, performance, usability, and sustainable development.

4R022's public projects form the part of the brand that meets users and the software community. A significant portion of these projects is offered **free of charge** and aims to provide practical solutions to problems encountered in everyday use.

However, it would not be accurate to define 4R022 solely through its published applications.

Beyond its existing projects, the brand is a **working framework** that also encompasses development processes, personal work tools, internal technical components, and future project goals.

Decisions about which parts of this structure will be published and which will remain private are made **deliberately**.

> **Public projects are the visible part of 4R022; they are not obliged to represent the brand in its entirety.**

---

## 03 · SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AND ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

I do not view software development merely as adding features or producing new applications.

The true value of a project should be assessed through its **functionality, stability, performance, maintainability**, and how well it lends itself to further development.

For this reason, I value technical documentation, version history, bug assessment, testing processes, and performance improvements throughout development.

I am aware that as software grows, so do the details that must be managed. New features can complicate the existing structure; therefore, refinement, optimization, and the re-evaluation of existing decisions are as necessary as development itself.

My goal is not only to produce projects that work today, but to build **systems that can still be developed tomorrow**.

**Modularity and flexibility** are also important parts of this approach. I do not believe every feature must be mandatory for every user. Where appropriate, optional components can serve different usage needs and help manage the system's resource consumption.

I measure technical success not only by how comprehensive a system is, but also by **how efficiently it uses its resources**.

---

## 04 · DATA ANALYSIS AND SYSTEMS THINKING

Data analysis is an important field for evaluating systems and understanding events.

Data alone does not present the whole picture. For a sound evaluation, one must understand the data's **source, context, reliability**, and its relationship with other information.

For this reason, in an analytical approach it is important to distinguish **observation from inference, and verified information from assumption**.

When examining a system's behavior, evaluating the relationships between components and possible causes, rather than focusing on a single symptom, can lead to more reliable conclusions.

The same approach can be applied in different fields such as software development, performance troubleshooting, system administration, and digital security.

For me, analytical thinking is not merely about possessing a large amount of information. What matters most is **interpreting available information correctly, knowing its limits**, and **using the results responsibly**.

---

## 05 · DIGITAL SECURITY AND PROTECTION PRINCIPLES

In the digital world, simply hiding is not enough.

Security requires understanding systems, assessing risks, controlling access, protecting data, and being prepared for problems that may arise.

A system that looks simple from the outside is not necessarily technically simple or lacking protection mechanisms. Conversely, a complex architecture is not, by itself, a guarantee of security.

Reliability is achieved through **proper design, correct configuration, controlled access, regular assessment**, and the implementation of necessary safeguards.

In my private work under 4R022 as well, I consider **protecting privacy and access boundaries** to be important.

I believe I have no obligation to make all of my personal workspaces public. At the same time, I care about providing information that is as clear and accurate as possible about the projects I do make public.

**Privacy and transparency can be applied in different domains.** Explaining a project's function and terms of use does not require publishing all of its internal details.

---

## 06 · ANONYMITY AND PERSONAL PRIVACY

Personal privacy is, for me, neither a temporary preference nor a promotional strategy.

My real identity, age, location, and the information about my private life that I do not share are part of my personal space. Whether or not to disclose such information is **my decision**.

My position on this is clear: I am open to the examination of my public work; I **do not accept unauthorized intrusion** into my private life.

Reviewing my projects, offering technical opinions, or criticizing my published work is a different kind of behavior from attempting to obtain my personal information.

The former is a natural part of the public creative process. The latter may violate the boundaries of personal privacy.

The fact that my personal information is not public does not mean that permission has been given to investigate or publish it.

I am not obliged to explain the limits of my anonymity in response to every request. The information I choose to share is public; **the information I do not share does not become public on its own**.

## 07 · 4R022'S PRIVATE STRUCTURE AND AUTHORIZATION POLICY

The founder and authorized administrator of 4R022's private systems is **Kaan Kross**.

There is a **clear distinction** between the brand's public projects and its internal systems, private work tools, and unpublished projects.

An application being public does not grant authority to access unrelated private resources or access-restricted components. Permission to review a repository cannot be interpreted as authorization over other accounts, servers, files, or systems.

**Unauthorized access is not accepted.**

Attempts to access private resources without permission, bypass protections, obtain data, or compromise the integrity of systems may, depending on the circumstances, be technically examined and reported to the appropriate platforms or competent authorities.

I reserve the right to determine the scope of information that may be disclosed about 4R022's internal architecture, private projects, and personal working methods.

Getting to know the brand as a whole does not require every internal detail to be made public. Likewise, making assumptions about an undisclosed component does not create any authority over that component.

> **Curiosity is not permission to access. Public openness is not unlimited access. Authority is not obtained by assumption.**

These principles express 4R022's approach to protecting its private working environments.

---

## 08 · 4R022'S OWN PRINCIPLES

4R022 is a structure that evolves in line with the rules and boundaries I have adopted in carrying out my work.

These principles are founded on **mutual respect, responsibility, the protection of privacy**, and taking necessary measures against unauthorized intrusion.

We treat people who communicate with us respectfully, honestly, and constructively with the same seriousness. Technical criticism and differing views are natural and valuable, as long as boundaries are respected.

By contrast, **harassment, threats, deliberate harm, unauthorized access, or violations of personal privacy** are not behaviors that will be normalized.

In such cases, our approach is not to retaliate arbitrarily or engage in personal disputes, but to assess the nature of the incident, preserve necessary evidence, restrict access, and use appropriate channels.

4R022 has its own principles. These principles do not grant anyone unlimited power; they require that decisions be made within defined limits and responsibilities.

> **Respect is mutual. Access is based on authorization. Responsibility is a principle that must be upheld under all circumstances.**

## 09 · LONG-TERM VISION

My goals in technology are not limited to a single application or a particular product.

I want to develop software that meets different needs, advance my existing projects, and, where appropriate, enable these projects to work together with more comprehensive systems.

These goals require developing new projects over time, improving existing architectures, and building expertise across different technical fields.

I do not measure long-term success by visibility alone. A brand's lasting value also depends on **the quality of its products, the consistency of its development processes, its technical expertise**, and the real-world use of the solutions it offers.

For this reason, my priority is to strengthen the foundations of 4R022, improve my existing work, and build a structure capable of producing more comprehensive projects in the future.

The scope and scale of future projects will be determined by the work accomplished and the results achieved.

## 10 · FINAL PUBLIC STATEMENT

I am **Kaan Kross**.

As the founder of 4R022, I continue my work in software development, system design, data analysis, and digital security.

My public projects constitute the examinable part of my approach to creation and my technical work. My private workspaces are protected within the boundaries of personal privacy and authorization.

Questions may be asked about my work, my published projects may be evaluated, and my technical decisions may be criticized. However, this does not mean that my private information or internal systems are open to unlimited access.

The future of 4R022 will continue to be shaped by the projects I develop and the work I deliver.

My focus is on **continuous improvement, technical competence, systematic work, and creating long-term value**.

> **Personal privacy is a choice. System security is a responsibility. Technical competence is judged by the work one delivers.**
